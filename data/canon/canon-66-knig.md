# The 66 books of the main canon

**English** | [Русский](canon-66-knig.ru.md)

Synodal names and abbreviations; the order of the main list.
Deuterocanonical books (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach,
Baruch, the Letter of Jeremiah, 1–3 Maccabees, 2–3 Esdras, the Prayer of
Manasseh and insertions) are not part of this list and are marked as
non-canonical.
