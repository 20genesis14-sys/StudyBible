StudyBible — a cross-platform app for Bible reading and study. Everything works offline: no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.

<b>Bundled content:</b> Synodal Russian Bible (1876), KJV 1769 with Strong's numbers, and OpenBible cross-references — ready out of the box.

<b>Features:</b>
• Reading and study modes
• Translation comparison
• Strong's lexicon
• Cross-references
• Notes, bookmarks, highlights
• Text-to-speech (system voices)
• Voice memos attached to notes
• Import of .sb/.sbz modules — single files or .zip bundles

<b>Permissions:</b> the only optional permission is the microphone, used solely for recording voice memos attached to your notes. The app never goes online — the build has no INTERNET permission.

Additional translations and reference works are imported as modules. Each module carries its own license and attribution, shown in the app.

Rust core, Flutter UI. MIT OR Apache-2.0.
